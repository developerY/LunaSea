## Brand & Style

The brand visual language merges high-end marine biotechnology with celestial tranquility. It caters to a discerning, luxury-conscious clientele seeking transformative, science-backed skincare grounded in oceanic extracts and bio-pearl infusions.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses refined **Minimalism** with luminous, tactile **Glassmorphism**:
- Soft, pearl-dusted surfaces with subtle atmospheric light diffusion.
- Generous, meditative negative space evoking calm tides and moonlit horizons.
- Delicate borders reflecting iridescent mother-of-pearl refractions.
- Restrained, cinematic compositions centered around editorial micro-copy and clean botanical geometry.

## Colors

The palette balances mineral marine tones with delicate, iridescent celestial highlights against an organic pearl-sand foundation:

- **Primary (`#27484B`) — Deep Abyssal Teal:** Used for high-contrast typography, primary calls-to-action, structural outlines, and critical brand grounding.
- **Secondary (`#8FAEA8`) — Seafoam Mist:** Used for subtle interactive fills, secondary icons, selected chips, and muted accents.
- **Tertiary (`#E0BFC8`) — Sea Salt Blush:** Reserved for delicate callouts, active indicators, high-illumination focus states, and ingredient callouts.
- **Neutral (`#F8F6F1`) — Sandy Pearl Cream:** Serves as the global backdrop, establishing a warm, non-clinical luxury canvas.

### Contextual Accents
- **Celestial Periwinkle (`#CCD6EB`):** Applied across delicate micro-borders and soft ambient backdrop glow pools.
- **Mother-of-Pearl Overlay (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`):** Used for frosted glass panels with backplate blur filters.

## Typography

The type system juxtaposes the neoclassical grace of **Playfair Display** with the geometric, airy clarity of **Outfit**:

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces literary depth and luxury editorial weight. Kept strictly at regular (400) and medium (500) weights to preserve delicate stem contrast.
- **Body & Captions:** Outfit in light (300) and regular (400) weights provides modern readability and openness, counteracting the ornate qualities of the serif.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** All labels are uppercase Outfit with tracking ranging from `+0.12em` to `+0.16em`, lending architectural balance to ingredient labels, rituals, and navigational headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max canvas constraint: `1440px`), compressing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** `margin` of `4rem` with `1.5rem` gutters allows content panels to breathe against soft pearl gradients. Generous element gaps (`space-xl`) separate editorial storytelling from functional commerce.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Margins pull into `2rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column formulas gracefully collapse into paired modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Margins contract to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters. Carousels utilize bleed margins (`margin-mobile` offset) to let glassmorphic cards peek past the viewport edge.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews heavy drop shadows in favor of translucent stratification, subtle environmental diffusion, and iridescent rim illumination:

- **Surface Level 0 (Ground Canvas):** Matte, warm pearl tone (`#F8F6F1`) punctuated by ambient linear blurs of soft periwinkle and blush.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Frosted glass panels using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` with backdrop blur filters (`16px` to `24px`) and an iridescent border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` layered with a second shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals & Flyouts):** Dense translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) over `32px` backdrop blur, bound by an ethereal rim glow (`0 16px 48px -8px rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.08)`, `0 0 0 1px rgba(224, 191, 200, 0.4)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Tooltips & Popovers):** Solid crisp pearl with a distinct oceanic shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The design system relies on balanced organic curvatures that mirror sea glass, tumbled pebbles, and water droplets:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for micro-elements, input fields, checkboxes, and badge tags.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to product cards, formula breakdown cards, and imagery containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Reserved for hero banners, bottom sheets, and promotional ritual containers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved for primary interactive buttons, category filter pills, and navigation bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill shape, filled with Abyssal Teal (`#27484B`), typography set in label-md uppercase (`#F8F6F1`). Hover triggers a smooth lift via seafoam ambient backglow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Ethereal Frosted):** Translucent white surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`) with a `1px` border of `rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.2)` and text in primary teal.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No fill; text styled with subtle bottom rule that expands on hover.

### Product & Ritual Cards
- Composed of frosted semi-translucent backdrops (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`), rounded to `1rem` (`16px`).
- Subtle mother-of-pearl hairline stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`).
- Media area retains a `4:5` aspect ratio with rounded inner corners (`12px`) and soft sea-spray background vignettes.

### Chips & Badges
- **Formulation Badges:** Pill-shaped, delicate seafoam mist tint (`rgba(143, 174, 168, 0.2)`), containing micro-text in primary teal with `0.14em` letter-spacing.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `1px` translucent border. In active states, transitions to blush (`rgba(224, 191, 200, 0.35)`) with primary teal typography.

### Input Fields
- Filled with semi-transparent ivory pearl (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), rounded to `0.5rem`, featuring a muted border (`rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.15)`).
- Focused state transforms the border to iridescent periwinkle (`#CCD6EB`) with an ambient teal/blush double ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(143, 174, 168, 0.2)`).
- Floating labels transition upward using label-sm specs.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox uses `4px` corner rounding; radio uses full circle.
- Inactive state: `1px solid rgba(39, 72, 75, 0.3)` on transparent ground.
- Selected state: Filled with Abyssal Teal (`#27484B`) displaying a crisp white micro-glyph or inner pearl dot.

### Specialized Component: Marine Formulation Accordion
- Clean borders separating key botanical sections (e.g., Deep Sea Algae, Bio-Pearl Peptides).
- Expand/collapse actions are indicated by minimalist `+` / `—` icons set in Sea Salt Blush (`#E0BFC8`).
- Expanded content sits over a faint seafoam-tinted inner wash.
